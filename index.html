Our story

Brown Pizza Cafe

Started in 2022 on Baypas Road near Kamal Takes Chauraha, Brown Pizza Cafe began as a small wood-fired pizza counter and grew into a full pure-veg kitchen — pizzas, Chinese, pasta, burgers, wraps, shakes and mocktails, all made fresh to order.

Every pizza is hand-stretched and baked to order, our Chinese counter fires up fresh Hakka and Schezwan, and our shake counter is where most regulars end their visit. Delivery runs across Palia Kalan Kheri — just call, WhatsApp, or order right from this menu.
